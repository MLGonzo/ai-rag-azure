# Starter Repair Kit Lending Notes

Harbor Hill lends small starter repair kits to members who want to finish a
simple project at home. The lending program is intended for low-risk household
repairs such as tightening cabinet hinges, replacing a bicycle reflector, or
measuring a shelf before cutting new material at the workshop.

Each starter repair kit includes:

- a six-in-one screwdriver;
- a small adjustable wrench;
- a tape measure;
- a pencil;
- painter's tape;
- a pair of safety glasses;
- a printed project checklist.

Kits do not include blades, power tools, glue, paint, solvents, batteries, or
hardware with sharp points. Members should use the kit for planning and light
repair only.

A member may borrow one kit for seven calendar days. The kit is due back by the
end of the workshop session on the seventh day. If the workshop is closed on the
due date, the kit is due during the next open session. A member who needs more
time can request one seven-day extension before the original due date.

The lending shelf has three labeled totes:

- Blue tote: Kit A, checked before each Saturday session.
- Green tote: Kit B, checked before each Tuesday session.
- Orange tote: Kit C, reserved for first-time borrowers.

Members sign the lending card when a kit leaves the building and again when it
returns. The workshop asks for a refundable 20 dollar deposit, but the deposit
can be waived by the shift lead for members using the volunteer membership
option.

If a tool is missing or damaged, members should tell the shift lead when the kit
is returned. The goal is to keep the kit safe and complete, not to surprise
borrowers with hidden fees.
