# Safety And Orientation Guide

All new Harbor Hill members complete a short orientation before using the bench
room. Orientation covers exits, first aid supplies, cleanup expectations, and
how to ask for help from a shift lead.

The basic orientation is required for every member. It takes about 20 minutes
and can be completed during any Tuesday or Thursday open session. Members who
only visit open lab night do not need orientation unless they start a project.

Some activities need extra approval:

- The soldering station requires the basic orientation plus a five-minute heat
  safety check.
- The hand drill requires the basic orientation plus a demonstration with scrap
  material.
- The sewing machine requires the basic orientation plus a threading check.

Youth visitors ages 13 through 17 may participate with a parent, guardian, or
approved program leader present. Youth visitors may use hand tools after basic
orientation, but they may not use the soldering station, hand drill, or sewing
machine without direct adult supervision and shift lead approval.

Closed-toe shoes are required in the bench room. Safety glasses are required
when cutting, drilling, hammering, or working near someone who is doing those
tasks. Food and drink stay at the clean table and should never be placed on the
bench room work surfaces.

Projects must fit on one workbench and must be removed at the end of the
session unless the shift lead approves overnight storage. Harbor Hill does not
store fuel, wet paint, chemical solvents, pressurized containers, or damaged
lithium batteries.

The final cleanup step is called "reset the bench." Members put tools back in
their labeled places, sweep small debris, wipe the work surface, and tell the
shift lead about anything broken or missing.
