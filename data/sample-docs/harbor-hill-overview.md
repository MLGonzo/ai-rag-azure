# Harbor Hill Community Workshop Overview

Harbor Hill Community Workshop is a fictional neighborhood workspace used in
this repository as safe sample content for retrieval testing. The workshop helps
residents repair household items, learn basic tool skills, and share practical
maintenance knowledge.

The workshop is located at 17 Cedar Quay in the fictional Dockside
neighborhood. It is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM and
on Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The first Thursday of each month is an
open lab night from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Visitors can tour the space during open
lab without becoming members.

Membership costs 12 dollars per month. Residents can also cover membership by
volunteering for two approved workshop hours during the same month. The
volunteer option is meant for learners who want to contribute time instead of
paying the monthly fee.

The workshop has three main areas:

- The bench room, used for small repairs and hand tools.
- The clean table, used for paperwork, laptop setup, and low-dust projects.
- The materials shelf, used for donated fasteners, scrap wood, fabric, and
  reusable containers.

Harbor Hill keeps the lesson scope small. It does not run a commercial repair
service, accept hazardous materials, store medical equipment, or handle private
records for members. The sample content in this repo is invented for teaching
and should not be treated as operational guidance for a real workshop.

For questions in the fictional setting, members would contact
hello@harborhill.example. That address uses the reserved `.example` domain and
is not a real inbox.
